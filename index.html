What We Do

Roofing and home improvements, start to finish

From a full re-roof down to a set of bifolds or a new bathroom — one team, one point of contact, and no sub-contracting the finish out to someone else.

Our Speciality

Pitched Roofing & Re-Roofs

Full strip-and-recover, new battens and membrane, slate and concrete tile work, ridge and verge, leadwork and chimney flashing. Scaffold arranged as part of the job, and the site swept clean before we leave.

Flat Roofing

EPDM rubber and felt systems for garages, extensions and dormers, with proper falls, trims and edge detail so water goes where it should.

Bathrooms

Full bathroom and shower room fits — panelling, trays, screens, tiling, sanitaryware and lighting, finished as a complete room rather than a part job.

Bifold & Patio Doors

Supply and install of aluminium bifolds and sliding doors, including opening up, lintels, making good and a level threshold that runs smooth.

Fascias, Soffits & Guttering

UPVC fascia and soffit replacement with new gutters and downpipes — a straightforward way to tidy up a roofline and stop water tracking down the wall.

Chimneys & Leadwork

Repointing, re-flaunching, flue installs and new lead flashings — the details that most roof leaks actually start at.
