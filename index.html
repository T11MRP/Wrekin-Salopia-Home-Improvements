What We Do

Roofing first — and everything that goes with it

Roofing is where Wrekin & Salopia started and it is still the bulk of what we do. Rooflines, glazing and interiors came later because customers kept asking — so now you can get the lot done by one team instead of lining up four. We cover all aspects of trade work, domestic and commercial.

01

Pitched Roofs & Re-Roofs

The core of what Wrekin & Salopia do. Full re-roofs, new tiling and slating, storm damage repairs, ridge and verge work, valleys, felt and batten replacement, and one-off repairs where a roof has a specific fault rather than needing stripping. We work in tile and natural slate, strip back to the timbers so we can see what we are actually dealing with, and put right anything hidden underneath before the new covering goes on. Every roof gets scaffolded properly and left watertight at the end of each day.

02

Flat Roofing

EPDM rubber and felt systems for garages, extensions and dormers. Properly falls-checked so water runs off rather than sitting, with new trims and edge detail fitted as part of the job.

03

Fascias, Soffits & Guttering

Full roofline replacement in uPVC. Rotten timber removed, new boards and vented soffits fitted, and guttering run and sealed so the water goes where it should instead of down the wall.

04

Chimneys & Leadwork

Repointing, rebuilds, flue liners, cappings and removals, plus code-appropriate lead flashings to chimneys, abutments and valleys — the details that cause most of the leaks we get called out to.

05

Glazing — Bifolds, Doors & Windows

Bifold and patio doors, replacement windows and external doors. Openings formed and made good where needed, so the brickwork and plaster are finished rather than left for someone else.

06

Bathrooms & Interiors

Full bathroom fit-outs and general interior work, start to finish.
